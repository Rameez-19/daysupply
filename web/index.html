Early warnings

Three signals, three responses

Early warning a clinical presentation that precedes the treatment course it drives — attendance, confirmed malaria, infectious admissions.
Happening now the visit and the dispensing move together; a chronic review is the repeat prescription. Information, not warning.
Planned campaign a calendar event, not an outbreak. Albendazole moves 22.64× on National Deworming Day — flagging that as an emergency would be the clearest false positive this system could produce.

A month is a surge when the modified z-score of its residual against the pooled seasonal pattern reaches 3.5, demand is at least 1.5× expected, and at least 100 clinical events were recorded. We do not predict outbreaks; we detect that one has begun, earlier and more reliably than a 3-sigma rule — which on twelve monthly observations cannot fire above 3.175 at all.
